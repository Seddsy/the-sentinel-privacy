## Information used by Sentinel

- **Identity and permissions.** Sentinel uses Root user and community IDs, display names, and role memberships to identify members and authorize features. It shows role and channel names and IDs in the owner's configuration screens.
- **Configuration.** Sentinel stores selected role and channel IDs, staff role mappings, monitored channel choices, verification rules, filter lists, and routines. When the owner provisions isolation resources, Root stores the role, group, channel, and access rules as community resources.
- **Monitored chat and moderation.** The owner selects which Root text channels Sentinel monitors. From that point Sentinel may store messages, edits, removal status, member and channel IDs, and timestamps from those channels, including evidence retained for cases. It uses this data for rule evaluation, member history, sanctions, and audit records. The configured isolation channel is excluded from monitoring.
- **Verification, reports, and notices.** Sentinel stores rule acceptance and access decisions, private help requests, whistleblowing reports, and app notifications. The information is shared within the app according to the member's access and the community's configured staff permissions. A Root push can ask a member to open Sentinel to read their notification.

## Retention and deletion

Sentinel keeps the community configuration and records in the app datastore. This pre-release has no automatic age-based deletion schedule. Removing a monitored channel stops new collection from that channel. Previously stored messages and evidence can remain available to authorized staff. Root manages the hosted datastore and its backups. The community owner can manage or remove the role, group, channel, and access rules separately in Root. [Root persistence and backups](https://docs.rootapp.com/docs/app-docs/design/save-data-early-and-often/)

To ask for access, correction, or deletion of information associated with Sentinel, contact [Root Support](https://www.rootapp.com/support) or [email the operator](mailto:seddsylive@gmail.com?subject=The%20Sentinel%20privacy) with the subject **The Sentinel privacy**. Include the Root community and enough detail to identify the records involved. The operator will route requests about Root-controlled app data to Root and assist under its instructions.
